# Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **100 sales records** to identify sales performance by product, category, customer, region, and month.

The project was completed using **Excel, MySQL, and Power BI** to demonstrate data analysis, SQL querying, data visualization, and business insight generation.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data organization and PivotTables
* **MySQL** – Data analysis and SQL queries
* **Power BI** – Interactive sales dashboard
* **GitHub** – Project documentation and version control

## 📈 Key Analysis

The analysis focused on:

* Total sales performance
* Sales by product
* Sales by customer
* Sales by category
* Sales by region
* Monthly sales performance

## 💡 Business Insights

* **Total Sales:** The business generated **771,400** in total sales across 100 records.
* **Top Category:** **Electronics** generated **588,000**, making it the strongest-performing category.
* **Top Region:** **Gauteng** recorded the highest sales at **193,500**.
* **Lowest Region:** **North West** recorded the lowest sales at **20,400**, indicating an opportunity for growth.
* **Monthly Performance:** **February** had the highest monthly sales at **235,900**, while April recorded the lowest at **102,900**.
* **Product and Customer Analysis:** Product and customer-level analysis was used to identify high-performing areas and support better sales decisions.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing and inventory strategies on the strong-performing **Electronics** category.
* Investigate opportunities to increase sales in lower-performing regions, particularly **North West**.
* Prepare promotions and sufficient inventory for the stronger **January–February** sales period.
* Use customer and product performance data to support targeted promotions and customer retention.
* Investigate the significant decline in sales during **April**.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides a visual overview of sales performance using charts and key sales metrics.

The dashboard helps users quickly identify trends and compare performance across different sales dimensions.
